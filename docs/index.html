    Mesonet Air Quality

    PurpleAir sensors at Montana Mesonet stations

    What this shows

    Low-cost PurpleAir PA-II sensors at Montana Mesonet stations. Each dot shows the latest hourly NowCast AQI for fine particulate matter (PM2.5), using the EPA's May 2024 breakpoints. The number on the dot is the AQI, so the colors are never the only cue.

    Data are published nightly, through the end of the previous day (UTC). This is not a real-time smoke alert. For current conditions, see AirNow Fire and Smoke Map.

    How to use it

    • Click a dot, or use Open a station, for its chart and downloads.
    • Choose a variable and a time range. Ranges up to 7 days can show the raw 2-minute A/B channels.
    • Keyboard: the station picker, and the station table that follows the map, reach every sensor.

    Share or bookmark

    The address bar always holds the current view: station, variable, range, map position and theme. Share copies it. This map has no single-key shortcuts, so ?kbd=off has nothing to turn off.

    Data

    PM2.5 is corrected with the EPA US-wide correction (Barkjohn et al. 2021, with the 2023 smoke extension). Hours where the two laser channels disagree are screened out. These are corrected low-cost sensor data, not regulatory measurements. The full archive is open at data2.climate.umt.edu/mesonet/air-quality. Source: mesonet-aq.
